Our Story

A classic café
with a modern soul

Café Aroma is a Greek family-owned neighborhood treasure nestled at 55 Wall Street in the heart of Norwalk. From the moment you walk in, you feel the warmth — the kind that comes not from marketing, but from genuine passion for good coffee.

Owner Paul and his family have built something rare: a place where locals feel at home and visitors feel they've found a secret. Coffee is the heart of everything — from silky Lavazza espresso to the iconic Freddo that keeps everyone coming back.
